# Free For Dev — Free Services for Developers

- **Repository**: [https://github.com/ripienaar/free-for-dev](https://github.com/ripienaar/free-for-dev)
- **Description**: Comprehensive list of software (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS) that provide free tiers for developers.

## Major Free Tiers
- **Hosting & Cloud**: Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages & Workers, Fly.io, Railway.
- **Databases**: Neon (Serverless Postgres), Supabase, MongoDB Atlas, Upstash (Redis).
- **Authentication**: Clerk, Supabase Auth, Auth0, Firebase Auth.
- **Monitoring & Errors**: Sentry, Datadog (free tier), Logtail, Better Stack.
