# Open Design — DeepSeek Harness Design Plugin & Claude Design Alternative

- **Repository**: [https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design)
- **Description**: Local-first desktop app and AI coding agent design engine. Generates prototypes, landing pages, dashboards, slides, images, and video with real HTML/PDF/PPTX/MP4 export.

## Key Capabilities
- **Multi-Agent Compatibility**: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, DeepSeek Harness, OpenCode, Antigravity.
- **Design Tokens & Systems**: Over 160+ specialized design skills included in the vault.
- **Direct Export**: Generates functional React, Tailwind, and HTML deliverables ready for production.
- **Generative Media**: Integrated fal.ai, Replicate, and MiniMax endpoints for assets.
